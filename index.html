01 — 06
PORTRAIT STUDY / PLACEHOLDER
JALAL KASHANITEHRAN / IRAN

I design spaces that give stories a physical presence.

I'm Jalal Kashani, a scenographer and production designer working across theatre, advertising, television and short film. I develop visual environments that support the story, shape atmosphere and respond to the practical demands of production.

My professional journey began in theatre, where I studied stage design and developed an approach to storytelling through space, composition, light, materials and visual atmosphere. Alongside design, I gained hands-on experience in stage and production departments.

My work has expanded into commercial production, short films, live events and visual communication. I collaborate with directors and production teams to translate a creative brief into a clear spatial idea — from early visual direction through the finished set.

Narrative first. Space with purpose. Details that hold the frame.

01 / RESEARCH & SKETCH

Every space begins with a question.

The first stage is observation: reading the story, exploring references, drawing relationships and finding the visual language of a space.

02 / SPATIAL DEVELOPMENT

An idea becomes a structure.

Sketches evolve into spatial studies, scale, material tests and models. Composition begins to meet practical constraints.

03 / LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE

Light, texture, color and perspective turn the design into an environment that can hold a performance, a camera or an audience.
