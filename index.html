About

01 / PROFILE
Creating visual worlds through space, material, composition and imagination.

I am Jalal Kashani, a production designer, set and costume designer with an academic background in Performing Arts and Set Design.

My professional experience includes work across advertising, brand films, visual campaigns, theatre and short films. I approach each project through visual research, spatial thinking and the development of a coherent visual language.

My interest lies in the way designed environments, objects, textures and costumes contribute to storytelling and shape the viewer's experience.
