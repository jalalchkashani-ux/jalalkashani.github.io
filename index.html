JALAL KASHANI TEHRAN / IRAN

I create spaces that give stories a physical presence.

I am Jalal Kashani, a scenographer and production designer working across theatre, advertising, television and short films. My practice focuses on building visual environments that support narrative, shape atmosphere and serve the practical needs of production.

My professional path began in theatre, where I studied set design and developed an approach to storytelling through space, composition, light, materials and visual atmosphere. I also gained hands-on experience in stage and production departments.

Over time, my work expanded into commercial productions, short films, live events and visual communication. I collaborate with directors and production teams to translate creative briefs into spatial concepts — from early visual research to the final set.

Narrative first. Space with intention. Details that stay in the frame.
